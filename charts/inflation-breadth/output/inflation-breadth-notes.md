Share of categories
:   The share of the 177 categories of consumer spending in the Dallas Fed's trimmed mean whose prices rose more than 3 percent over the past 12 months. Each category counts equally, whatever its share of spending.

Dashed line
:   The average from 2000 to 2019.

::: {.chart-source}
