::: {.chart-notes}

Today
:   The 12-month change in the consumer price index, from January 2015.

1970s
:   The same, from June 1967 to May 1983, shifted forward 571 months, or 47 years and 7 months. The top axis gives its years.

October 2025
:   BLS collected no prices that month, during the federal shutdown. The index is filled with the geometric mean of September and November 2025, so the 12-month changes for October 2025 and October 2026 are estimates.
