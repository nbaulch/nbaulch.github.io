::: {.chart-notes}

Headline
:   All consumer spending.

Core
:   Excluding food and energy.

Excluding energy goods
:   Excluding gasoline and other fuels, but keeping food and energy services.

Market-based core
:   Core, excluding prices the government estimates rather than observes, such as for free bank services.

Market-based core excluding housing
:   Market-based core, also excluding rents.

Median
:   The price change in the middle of the distribution each month.

New York Fed trend
:   A model estimate of trend inflation from 17 sectors.

Trimmed mean
:   The average price change after dropping the largest and smallest each month.

Dashed line
:   The Federal Reserve's 2 percent inflation goal.

Shading
:   Distance from the 2 percent goal, orange above and blue below.
