::: {.chart-notes}

Gap
:   The 12-month change in core PCE prices minus the 12-month change in core CPI prices. Both leave out food and energy.

Gap excluding portfolio management and software
:   The same gap with two items taken out of core PCE: fees for managing investments and giving investment advice, which are in PCE but not CPI, and computer software and accessories, which weigh far more in PCE than in CPI.

Dashed line
:   The average gap from 2011 to 2019.
