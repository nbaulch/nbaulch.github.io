::: {.chart-notes}

Gap
:   The 12-month change in core PCE prices minus core CPI prices, both excluding food and energy.

Computers and software
:   Computers, peripherals, and software, which weigh more in PCE.

Car insurance
:   Motor vehicle insurance, which weighs more in CPI.

Medical services
:   Health care services, including care paid for by employers and government, which only PCE covers.

Other
:   Everything else in core, including housing, which weighs more in CPI.

Change from the 2011–19 average
:   Each series, and each item's contribution to core PCE minus its contribution to core CPI, less its average from 2011 to 2019.
